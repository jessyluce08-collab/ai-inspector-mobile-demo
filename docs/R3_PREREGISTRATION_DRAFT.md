Outcomes: counts/fractions and denominators exactly as protocol. Separate file intake, sessions and planned slots. Missing files require the external enrollment register; never infer return rate from exports alone. Technical flags do not disappear from denominators. T,U and missing are distinct. Controls are not an observer accuracy test. Report higher-imposed-blur direction separately from human judgment; do not call it perceptual ground truth.

Planned analyses are descriptive: individual counts/rates, equal-weight participant mean missing/U rates, full response distributions by condition/source, control apparent-difference reports and explicit conditional A/B denominators. Pooled counts are not independent observations. No significance testing, population CI, power or confirmatory endpoint. Device strata, source/condition patterns and arm asymmetry are exploratory and reported only with cell counts. No arbitrary threshold retrospectively converts a finding into PASS. No adjustment for multiple testing is necessary because no family of inferential tests is planned; do not add selected p-values later.
