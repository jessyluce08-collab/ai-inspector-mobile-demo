Version **R3-FEASIBILITY-PROTOCOL-1**. Status: DRAFT FOR INVESTIGATOR COMPLETION; no recruitment, research collection or ethics approval. ## Objective and fixed task

Assess feasibility, reliable operation, file return/completeness and whether the existing photographic relative-blur task yields interpretable responses. This is a convenience feasibility pilot, not confirmatory perception science, analyzer validation, unwantedness/intent detection, facial quality or optical blur estimation. Prior Android/iOS successful runs are owner-reported engineering smoke tests, not instrumented geometry qualification or pilot observations. They will never be admitted retrospectively.

Investigator reviews applicable ethics/data-protection requirements and eligibility; no legal basis, exemption or review approval is invented here.

## Responses, controls and validity

A: image A perceived more blurred. B: image B perceived more blurred. T: no perceived difference after inspection. U: unable to judge. U is abstention; T is an actual perceptual response; never merge them or replace missing data with either. Imposed blur direction is manipulation metadata, not a human correctness label. The original photograph is not assumed objectively sharp.

Primary intake requires supported schema, locked build/code/manifest, exact seed-derived24slots and unique pair/trial/assignment identities, counts/digest consistency and original response/geometry invariants. Research files additionally require approved prospective pilot release, external consent/eligibility register and session start after authorization/registration/consent. Hashes do not establish authenticity, calibrated physical display or that a person actually attended.

Missing/corrupt/incompatible files remain in the private intake audit; no fabricated rows or silent deletion. No recovery by editing original JSON. Five-trial demos, technical smoke tests, preauthorization starts, missing consent/eligibility or withdrawn submissions are excluded from pilot analysis. Partial/stopped/technical-error sessions remain in feasibility denominators and preserve all24slots when structurally valid. Completed responses must form the UI's possible prefix. Conflicting files claiming the same session are quarantined together. Exact copies/export retries retain one deterministic representative; same code/new session retains the first chronologically eligible session, retaining later files/reasons in the ledger. Unknown independence is reported, not inferred from device/IP.

Within included sessions, every planned slot appears in summaries. Technical invalidation/visibility interruption makes a trial ineligible for perceptual-direction analysis, but raw A/B/T/U, missingness and flag counts remain. A/B/T under restricted geometry fails structural admission rather than being silently recoded. U under restricted geometry is legitimate and reported. Downsampling alone is retained/flagged, not automatically excluded. No minimum time, control-pass count, agreement cutoff or response-based participant exclusion. An A/B response on an identical control is reported as an apparent difference, never automatic participant incompetence.

## Outcomes and computations

Primary feasibility outcomes: received files, structurally valid files, conflicts/duplicates/exclusions; included complete/partial sessions; completed/24 and missing/24 by participant; U/24; technically flagged slots/24; and number of usable A/B/T judgments with explicit denominator. File-return fraction requires the organizer's prospective count of eligible nonwithdrawn consented codes; it cannot be inferred from received exports. Report that external denominator separately, with attempts/withdrawal/unknown return counts. Do not invent a zero for unavailable register information.

Secondary: A/B/T/U on four identical controls, A/B reports divided by four planned controls and separately by observed controls; ties/abstentions by condition/source/display status; number reporting higher-imposed blur in20 nonidentity planned pairs, plus conditional frequency among eligible nonidentity A/B responses. Report T/U/missing/flagged alongside every conditional figure. Same-source content effects and A/B arm asymmetry are exploratory.

Participant summaries and equal-weight participant mean rates are primary descriptive aggregation. Pooled trial counts are supplementary fixed-panel bookkeeping. Four sources and up to six convenience observers do not justify population inference, an unclustered binomial CI, p-values or treating144slots as independent. No confirmatory test, power claim or significance threshold is planned. No analyzer is executed or tuned; analyzer–human agreement is deferred exploratory work requiring a frozen analyzer and a separate clearly identified analysis, not a prerequisite to this pilot's feasibility report.

## Stopping, changes and limitations

Proposed operational rules requiring investigator approval: close invitations after six eligible consenting codes have been assigned, or14 consecutive calendar days after the first authorized session begins, whichever comes first. Do not replace nonreturns, partial sessions, ties, U, unexpected controls or disagreement. One attempt per observer; export retry is not a new attempt. Stop immediately for privacy breach, stimulus/build mismatch, missing consent or reproducible critical collection/presentation/export failure. Preserve evidence; resume only after documented owner decision/prospective deviation. No extensions based on response patterns. If fewer files arrive, report feasibility and stop, not an optimization cycle.

No numeric PASS threshold for feasibility is scientifically justified here. Rates and adverse cases inform the investigator's next decision. Software parser/file-size limits are engineering safeguards, not observer exclusions or scientific thresholds. Investigator may approve different operational limits only prospectively before first enrollment and update/register the locked documents within the single final verification ticket.

Limitations: four-source restricted content, convenience friends/volunteers, uncontrolled devices/viewing, accessible client-side transformation metadata, unknown nonreturn reasons, imperfect consent/identity verification and descriptive precision. No physical calibration, creator intent, universal unwanted-blur label, restoration authority, detector qualification or integration follows. All pre-pilot engineering runs are excluded.
